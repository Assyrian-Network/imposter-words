# Code of Conduct

This is a public word list. People should be able to propose and talk about Suret words without getting dragged into a fight.

## Do this

- Be decent to other contributors and to the language. Suret spelling and dialect differ by region. Argue the word, not the person.
- Give notes on the entry, not on who submitted it.
- Assume a typo or an unfamiliar dialect is a mistake, not a slight.
- Stay on the list, the data, and how we review it.

## Don't

- Harass, insult, or troll people in issues, PRs, or comments.
- Use slurs, sexual language, or anything that would not belong in a family game. The same bar as [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) applies to how we talk here.
- Post someone else's private information.
- Treat the repo like a dumping ground.

## If it happens

Maintainers can edit or close comments, commits, issues, and pull requests, and can warn or block people who keep crossing the line.

To report something privately, message the maintainers on GitHub. We will handle it quietly and quickly.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
